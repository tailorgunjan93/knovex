The AI brain

Today

Run it free, or bring your own

Run the AI 100% free on your own machine, or plug in a provider you already use (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Gemini). Either way, your documents never leave your laptop.

Coming soon

Zero setup — built-in AI

An upcoming release bundles Keepvidya's own on-device models — Shiva-Chat 2B & 7B — so Knovex just works out of the box. No accounts, no keys, nothing to install.

100% offline

Your data stays local

No account. No telemetry. Knovex works with Wi-Fi switched off. The only thing that ever leaves your machine is an LLM call — and only to the provider you chose.

Your knowledge bases and API keys are encrypted at rest. Delete the app and your data is just files you still own.
